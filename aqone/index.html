AqOne

Gabay sa Bawat Alon, Konektado sa Bawat Layon

An offline SOS system for municipal fishers. A fisher at sea can call for help and know that responders on shore received it, with no cell signal needed.

See the recognition Built inside the Guild

The problem

Municipal fishers in New Washington, Aklan, work far enough out that their phones lose signal. When something goes wrong at sea, there is no reliable way to call for help, and no way to know whether anyone heard.

Families and the local disaster office often learn about trouble only when a boat fails to come home.

My role

Lead developer on a student team. I lead the architecture, the backend and deployment, and I helped pitch AqOne at every competition below.

Technical details are kept private while the team prepares a patent application.

What AqOne does

In plain terms, for the people who use it.

  1. A fisher sends an SOS

    From a phone on the boat, even far beyond cell coverage.

  2. The call reaches shore

    Responders at the local disaster office see who needs help and where.

  3. Help confirms it's coming

    The fisher sees that responders received the call and when to expect them.
