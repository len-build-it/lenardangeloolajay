Why the Guild exists

Western Visayas needs more tech startups, and most of the activity sits in Iloilo. Before the Guild was formed, its founding members and their teams earned 15 recognitions in 2026. That showed students in Aklan can compete with anyone.

Most student projects stay in the competitions. The Guild's focus now is getting members' software into real use by fishers, farmers and communities in Aklan.

The startup teams already have mentors. What the Guild is building is the organization around them: structure, programs and a path from classroom project to company.

My role

Founder and president. I set up the Guild's structure and accreditation, lead its outreach to industry and government partners, and still build alongside the teams as lead developer of AqOne.

Faculty adviser: Edward S. Gumban, MIT
