How I build

1. Start with who it's for

Every build starts with people and a problem they actually have: fishers out of signal, families in flood zones.

2. Build for their conditions

If the people I build for have no signal, the software can't need one. Tools: Flutter, Dart, Riverpod, Drift and SQLite; Python, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, REST APIs, Docker; JavaScript, HTML, CSS, Java, Git and GitHub.

3. Put it in front of reality

A bench run or a slide deck isn't proof. I take builds outside and in front of judges and mentors, and treat what they find as the next task.

4. Bring others along

One student project won't change Aklan. I founded the ASU Developers' Guild so more of them make it past the competition stage.
