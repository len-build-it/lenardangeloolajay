DOST RSTW 2026 Champion

AqOne - Offline Maritime SOS Network ↗(opens in new tab)

Role: Lead Developer - Backend, Architecture & Deployment.

An offline SOS network for municipal fishers in New Washington, Aklan, who work beyond cellular reach. A fisher sends an SOS from their phone over local WiFi to a boat safety pod, which relays it over LoRa to a shore gateway, the backend, and an MDRRMO operations dashboard. Responders acknowledge and send an ETA back, and every alert is tracked as saved, relayed, delivered, and acknowledged. Built the FastAPI and PostgreSQL backend, the gateway ingest pipeline, and the deployment.

Stack: Flutter · FastAPI · PostgreSQL · ESP32 · LoRa

Honors: Champion, DOST Western Visayas RSTW Student Startup Competition 2026 · Most Innovative Solution, DTI ASPIRE Startup Bootcamp Demo Day 2026 · Top 4, DICT Philippine Startup Challenge XI (Region VI) · 2nd Place, AI Fest 2026 AI Hackathon (School-Based)
